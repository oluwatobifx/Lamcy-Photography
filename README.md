# Lamcy Photography & Videography

Capture · Create · Inspire.

A static website for a photography and videography studio. The home page is a hard-cover book: tap the cover to open it, turn the pages to browse the photo chapters, and tap any photo to view it full screen in 4K. A side booking panel lets visitors pick a service (photoshoot, videography, birthday shoot, baby shower shoot, video coverage, event planning & décor, flyer design), set options and duration, see the total price, and send the booking on WhatsApp.

## Editing

- `data.js`: contact details (WhatsApp number, email, city), photos, book pages and every service with its prices.
- `styles.css`, `app.js`, `index.html`: design and behaviour.

The WhatsApp number in `data.js` is a placeholder and prices are samples. Photos are free stock photos from Unsplash and are credited in the photo viewer.

Hosted on Vercel; every push to `main` redeploys.
